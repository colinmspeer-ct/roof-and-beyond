# Swatara Township
  
- Census geography 06000US4204375672. ACS 2020–2024 estimates 11,055 housing units, with a median building year of 1975, 56% detached and 23% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4204375672)
- ZIP areas use 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationship files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- NOAA recorded 0.88-inch hail near Rutherford and 0.75-inch hail south of Chambers Hill on September 21, 2024. [Storm record 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1209209) [Storm record 2](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1209210)
- Swatara’s Building and Codes office distinguishes routine maintenance from alterations and asks homeowners to call if unsure about a building or zoning permit. [Jurisdiction reference](https://www.dauphincounty.gov/about-the-county/municipalities) [Municipal guidance](https://swataratwp.com/codes-permits/)
