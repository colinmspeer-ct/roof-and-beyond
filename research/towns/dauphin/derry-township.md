# Derry Township
  
- Census geography 06000US4204318936. ACS 2020–2024 estimates 10,432 housing units, with a median building year of 1979, 50% detached and 22% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4204318936)
- ZIP areas use 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationship files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- NOAA describes June 2010 thunderstorm winds that caused widespread damage west of Campbelltown in Derry Township. [Storm record 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=227803)
- Derry Township publishes building permit materials. Contact Community Development to describe any structural work included in the scope.
