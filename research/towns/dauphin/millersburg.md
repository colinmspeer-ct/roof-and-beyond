# Millersburg
  
- Census geography 06000US4204349680. ACS 2020–2024 estimates 1,284 housing units, with a median building year of 1953, 50% detached and 24% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4204349680)
- ZIP areas use 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationship files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- NOAA logged a Millersburg downburst on June 29, 2024, with winds estimated at 95 mph. [Storm record 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1178792)
- Millersburg has a building height ordinance for new construction in its defined historic district area. Check the parcel and describe planned height changes to the borough. [Jurisdiction reference](https://www.dauphincounty.gov/about-the-county/municipalities) [Municipal guidance](https://www.millersburgpa.org/wp-content/uploads/Ordinances/2014/03-14%20building%20height.pdf)
