# Lower Paxton Township
  
- Census geography 06000US4204345056. ACS 2020–2024 estimates 22,669 housing units, with a median building year of 1980, 59% detached and 14% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4204345056)
- ZIP areas use 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationship files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- NOAA recorded hail east of Progress and at the northwest end of Rutherford on September 21, 2024. The entries concern the locations named in the reports. [Storm record 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1209204) [Storm record 2](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1209209)
- Lower Paxton Township publishes building code and permit contacts. Ask the codes office how it treats the actual reroof scope, including structural repairs.
