# Harrisburg
  
- Census geography 06000US4204332800. ACS 2020–2024 estimates 24,130 housing units, with a median building year of 1947, 14% detached and 43% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4204332800)
- ZIP areas use 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationship files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- The county hazard plan names Harrisburg among the communities affected by flash flooding during the remnants of Tropical Storm Debby in August 2024. [Storm record 1](https://www.dauphincounty.gov/docs/default-source/public-safety/hazard-mitigation-plan.pdf)
- Harrisburg’s Historic Architectural Review Board reviews projects under the city’s historic preservation rules. Ask the city how the proposed change would be reviewed. [Jurisdiction reference](https://www.dauphincounty.gov/about-the-county/municipalities) [Municipal guidance](https://www.harrisburgpa.gov/government/boards_commissions/architectural_review_board.php)
