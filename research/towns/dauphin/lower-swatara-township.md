# Lower Swatara Township
  
- Census geography 06000US4204345120. ACS 2020–2024 estimates 4,170 housing units, with a median building year of 1983, 67% detached and 14% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4204345120)
- ZIP areas use 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationship files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- The county hazard plan lists Lower Swatara Township in its entry for the remnants of Hurricane Ida in September 2021. [Storm record 1](https://www.dauphincounty.gov/docs/default-source/public-safety/hazard-mitigation-plan.pdf)
- Lower Swatara Township lists structural alterations and new porch roof framing among residential projects that need a building permit. [Jurisdiction reference](https://www.dauphincounty.gov/about-the-county/municipalities) [Municipal guidance](https://www.lowerswatara.org/code-enforcement/files/when-residential-permit-required)
- The township’s published 2015 IRC climate chart lists a ground snow load of 25 pounds per square foot. Confirm current design criteria with the township if roof framing changes. [Township climate chart](https://lowerswatara.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/climate_design_chart-1.pdf)
