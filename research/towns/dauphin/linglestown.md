# Linglestown
  
- Census geography 16000US4243672. ACS 2020–2024 estimates 2,804 housing units, with a median building year of 1969, 88% detached and 8% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=16000US4243672)
- ZIP areas use 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationship files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- The June 26, 2024 wind report east of Skyline View is a regional reference for Linglestown. NOAA describes snapped trees in the reported area. [Storm record 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1193202)
- Ask Lower Paxton Township’s building codes office about the scope for a Linglestown parcel in the township. [Jurisdiction reference](https://www.dauphincounty.gov/about-the-county/municipalities) [Municipal guidance](https://lowerpaxton-pa.gov/171/Building-Codes)
