# Highspire
  
- Census geography 06000US4204334664. ACS 2020–2024 estimates 1,436 housing units, with a median building year of 1964, 35% detached and 25% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4204334664)
- ZIP areas use 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationship files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- NOAA reported downed trees in Highspire during wind events on June 26 and September 21, 2024. [Storm record 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1193169) [Storm record 2](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1209239)
- Highspire publishes its building permit process and directs applicants to its Code Enforcement Office to schedule required inspections. [Jurisdiction reference](https://www.dauphincounty.gov/about-the-county/municipalities) [Municipal guidance](https://highspire.gov/permit-process-application/)
