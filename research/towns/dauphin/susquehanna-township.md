# Susquehanna Township
  
- Census geography 06000US4204375528. ACS 2020–2024 estimates 12,529 housing units, with a median building year of 1976, 51% detached and 20% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4204375528)
- ZIP areas use 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationship files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- NOAA recorded three-quarter-inch hail east of Progress on September 21, 2024. [Storm record 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1209204)
- Susquehanna Township accepts residential permit applications digitally or at its municipal office. The township asks applicants to include a site plan. [Jurisdiction reference](https://www.dauphincounty.gov/about-the-county/municipalities) [Municipal guidance](https://www.susquehannatwp.com/208/Permit-Information)
