# Springettsbury Township

- Census geography 06000US4213372992 (Springettsbury township). ACS 2020–2024 estimates 10,396 housing units, median construction year 1969, 66% detached and 11% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4213372992)
- NOAA logged a July 16, 2024 thunderstorm that downed dozens of trees in Springettsbury Township, including one that fell on a home. [Primary source 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1200469)
- Springettsbury’s Community Development Department handles building permits. Describe the existing roof and any new framing separately when you check your proposed scope. [Primary source 1](https://springettsbury.com/construction/community-development/)
- Station figures describe the York 3 SSW Pump Station and its 1991–2020 reporting period. [Regional Climate Center ACIS](https://www.rcc-acis.org/)
- Existing National Register references include East York Historic District, Pleasureville Historic District. [National Register database](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/nationalregister/database-research.htm)
