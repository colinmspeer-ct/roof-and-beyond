- Census geography 16000US4219208 (Dillsburg borough). ACS 2020–2024 estimates 1,079 housing units, median construction year 1983, 52% detached and 14% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=16000US4219208)
