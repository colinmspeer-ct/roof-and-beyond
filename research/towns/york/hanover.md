# Hanover

- Census geography 16000US4232448 (Hanover borough). ACS 2020–2024 estimates 8,029 housing units, median construction year 1956, 46% detached and 22% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=16000US4232448)
- NOAA recorded 1.75-inch hail on the south side of Hanover and in Parkville in September 2024. Ask for a ground-level look at vulnerable metal and an inspection of the affected roof slopes. [Primary source 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1209207)
- Hanover’s residential building permit form covers structural changes and additions. Describe any decking or framing changes when asking which paperwork applies. [Primary source 1](https://www.hanoverboroughpa.gov/239/Forms)
- Existing local weather context includes Hail, 29-MAR-09, reported location Hanover; Flood, 28-AUG-11, reported location Hanover. [NOAA Storm Events Database](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/storm-events-database/)
- Station figures describe the York 3 SSW Pump Station and its 1991–2020 reporting period. [Regional Climate Center ACIS](https://www.rcc-acis.org/)
- Existing National Register references include Hanover Historic District. [National Register database](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/nationalregister/database-research.htm)
