# York Township

- Census geography 06000US4213387056 (York township). ACS 2020–2024 estimates 13,508 housing units, median construction year 1982, 54% detached and 18% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4213387056)
- York Township’s Building and Zoning Department asks property owners to contact the township about permit requirements for the proposed work. Confirm the parcel before using the township instructions. [Primary source 1](https://yorktownshippa.gov/departments-2/building-and-zoning/)
- Station figures describe the York 3 SSW Pump Station and its 1991–2020 reporting period. [Regional Climate Center ACIS](https://www.rcc-acis.org/)
