# Spring Garden Township

- Census geography 06000US4213373168 (Spring Garden township). ACS 2020–2024 estimates 4,596 housing units, median construction year 1957, 74% detached and 19% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4213373168)
- Spring Garden’s permit page lists replacement of shingles or a rubber roof as permit-exempt. It lists roof framing replacement and a new roof over a porch or patio as building permit work. [Primary source 1](https://www.springgardentwp.org/building-permits/)
- Station figures describe the York 3 SSW Pump Station and its 1991–2020 reporting period. [Regional Climate Center ACIS](https://www.rcc-acis.org/)
