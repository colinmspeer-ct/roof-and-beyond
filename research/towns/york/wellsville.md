# Wellsville
  
- Census geography 06000US4213382200. ACS 2020–2024 estimates 110 housing units, with a median building year of 1938, 70% detached and 15% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4213382200)
- ZIP areas use 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationship files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- NOAA recorded strong thunderstorm winds near Dover in York County on June 29, 2024. The report describes the Dover area. [Storm record 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1189015)
- The National Register nomination documents the Wellsville Historic District. Ask the borough whether separate local rules apply to the proposed changes.
