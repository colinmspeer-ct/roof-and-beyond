# Stewartstown

- Census geography 16000US4274104 (Stewartstown borough). ACS 2020–2024 estimates 776 housing units, median construction year 1990, 53% detached and 21% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=16000US4274104)
- NOAA recorded downed trees and a fence southeast of Stewartstown in a June 5, 2024 thunderstorm. The reported wind was estimated near 60 mph. [Primary source 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1193247)
- Stewartstown publishes residential building and zoning applications for borough properties and directs homeowners to contact the borough office before applying. [Primary source 1](https://stewartstown.org/codes-enforcement-zoning/)
- Existing local weather context includes Hail, 30-JUL-19, reported location Stewartstown; Thunderstorm Wind, 30-JUL-19, reported location Stewartstown. [NOAA Storm Events Database](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/storm-events-database/)
