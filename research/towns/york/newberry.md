# Newberry Township

- Census geography 06000US4213353224 (Newberry township). ACS 2020–2024 estimates 6,396 housing units, median construction year 1984, 73% detached and 8% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=06000US4213353224)
- Station figures describe the York 3 SSW Pump Station and its 1991–2020 reporting period. [Regional Climate Center ACIS](https://www.rcc-acis.org/)
- Existing National Register references include Kise Mill Bridge Historic District. [National Register database](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/nationalregister/database-research.htm)
