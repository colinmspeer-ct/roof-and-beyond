# Mount Wolf

- Census geography 16000US4252056 (Mount Wolf borough). ACS 2020–2024 estimates 518 housing units, median construction year 1948, 63% detached and 24% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=16000US4252056)
- NOAA reports downed trees and power lines in Mount Wolf during a September 21, 2024 thunderstorm with winds estimated near 60 mph. [Primary source 1](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1209257)
- Existing local weather context includes Hail, 21-SEP-24, reported location Mt Wolf; Thunderstorm Wind, 21-SEP-24, reported location Mt Wolf. [NOAA Storm Events Database](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/storm-events-database/)
- Station figures describe the York 3 SSW Pump Station and its 1991–2020 reporting period. [Regional Climate Center ACIS](https://www.rcc-acis.org/)
