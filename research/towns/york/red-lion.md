# Red Lion

- Census geography 16000US4263840 (Red Lion borough). ACS 2020–2024 estimates 2,817 housing units, median construction year 1941, 30% detached and 36% attached. [ACS tables](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=16000US4263840)
- Red Lion directs applicants to submit building permits through its borough office. Its permit summary lists structural changes to a dwelling among work that requires a permit. [Primary source 1](https://redlionpa.org/departments-services/codes-enforcement-zoning/building-permits/) · [Primary source 2](https://redlionpa.org/forms-publications/borough-forms-publications/planning-zoning-building-permits-and-publications/)
- Existing local weather context includes Hail, 27-JUL-08, reported location Red Lion; Thunderstorm Wind, 28-SEP-06, reported location Red Lion. [NOAA Storm Events Database](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/storm-events-database/)
