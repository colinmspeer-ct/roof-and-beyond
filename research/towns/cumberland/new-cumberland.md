# New Cumberland

- ACS 2020–2024 geography 06000US4204153464 (New Cumberland borough). Estimated 3,536 units, median year 1953, 68% detached and 14% attached. [Census table extract](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=060%7C05000US42041)
- ZIP areas are 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationships](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- Winter storm declaration in January 2016 and Tropical Storm Debby in August 2024 are county-level records. [County hazard plan](https://www.cumberlandcountypa.gov/DocumentCenter/View/54454/TEXT---2025-Cumberland-County-HMP_Plan-111325)
- Municipal jurisdiction should be checked against the parcel. [County municipality directory](https://www.cumberlandcountypa.gov/1975/MDJ-Jurisdiction-by-Municipality)
- NOAA reports a tree down on a New Cumberland property during a June 2024 thunderstorm with gusts estimated at 60 mph. [NOAA event 1194813](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1194813)
- New Cumberland lists roof replacement on its Codes Enforcement and Zoning page and directs property owners to its building code official before the project. [Municipal permit guidance](https://newcumberlandborough.com/departments/codes-enforcement-and-zoning/)
