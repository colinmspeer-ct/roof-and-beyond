# Shippensburg

- ACS 2020–2024 geography 06000US4204170352 (the Cumberland County portion of Shippensburg Borough). Estimated 2,272 units, median year 1949, 33% detached and 9% attached. [Census table extract](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B25024,B25034,B25035&geo_ids=060%7C05000US42041)
- ZIP areas are 2020 ZCTA land overlaps. [Census relationships](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2020/geo/relationship-files.html)
- Winter storm declaration in January 2016 and Tropical Storm Debby in August 2024 are county-level records. [County hazard plan](https://www.cumberlandcountypa.gov/DocumentCenter/View/54454/TEXT---2025-Cumberland-County-HMP_Plan-111325)
- Municipal jurisdiction should be checked against the parcel. [County municipality directory](https://www.cumberlandcountypa.gov/1975/MDJ-Jurisdiction-by-Municipality)
- In the surrounding Cumberland Valley, NOAA recorded one-inch hail near Cleversburg in August 2023. In Shippensburg, identify which roof face was exposed before asking for a hail inspection. [NOAA event 1117899](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=1117899)
